# Boston Weekend Events

## 🎭 Event Summary

Here are 3 popular events happening in Boston this weekend:

---

## 1. Boston Calling Music Festival
**Date:** Saturday & Sunday  
**Location:** City Hall Plaza, Downtown Boston  
**Time:** 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM  

A premier outdoor music festival featuring indie rock, hip-hop, and electronic artists. This weekend's lineup includes performances on multiple stages with food vendors, craft beverages, and pop-up shops.

**Highlights:**
- Live performances across 3 stages
- Food court with local vendors
- Free entry (donations welcome)
- Family-friendly atmosphere

---

## 2. Boston Public Market Farmers Market & Artisan Fair
**Date:** Saturday & Sunday  
**Location:** Boston Public Market, Haymarket  
**Time:** 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM  

Browse fresh produce, local honey, baked goods, handmade crafts, and artisanal products from over 50 New England vendors. Perfect for weekend shopping and breakfast!

**Highlights:**
- Local farmers and artisans
- Farm-fresh produce and products
- Live cooking demonstrations
- Free admission

---

## 3. New England Aquarium Weekend Experience
**Date:** Saturday & Sunday  
**Location:** New England Aquarium, Atlantic Avenue  
**Time:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM  

Explore over 200 species of marine life including sea turtles, jellyfish, giant ocean tank exhibits, and the popular penguin colony. Interactive touch pools and educational programs throughout the day.

**Highlights:**
- Giant Ocean Tank with 200,000 gallons of water
- Interactive penguin exhibits
- 3D IMAX theater (additional fee)
- Educational programs every hour
- Gift shop and on-site dining
